Weinstein v. Copeland Case Overview

"Weinstein first acknowledged the unit was uninhabitable by funding displacement, then treated the same displacement as temporary, terminated housing support before safe resolution, pursued rent and possession, resisted accommodation obligations, and used litigation positions to convert a fair-housing emergency into leverage against a disabled tenant."
